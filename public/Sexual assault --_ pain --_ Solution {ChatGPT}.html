1. Neurological Distortion — Arousal and Aggression Systems
The human brain links sex and aggression more closely than we often acknowledge. Both involve activation of deep limbic structures — the amygdala, hypothalamus, and nucleus accumbens.   • Survivor Support: Trauma therapy, somatic and EMDR modalities, survivor networks, and medical care to reduce long-term damage.
